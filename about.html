About AutoRevive NG

AutoRevive NG was born from a simple idea: Nigerian car owners deserve access to modern, professional restyling services without the risk and illegality that comes with certain import schemes.

We help you upgrade older luxury SUVs and cars — G-Wagons, Porsches, BMWs, Lexus and more — with modern body kits, interiors, lighting and technology. Everything is done locally, by vetted professionals, with full compliance to Nigerian law.

Our approach is simple: compliance first, quality always, transparency throughout. We don't promise overnight transformations or illegal imports. We promise honest work, fair pricing, and a car you'll be proud to drive.

What we stand for

  • Legal, always. No reassembled vehicle imports. No 12+ year old cars. No shortcuts that put you at risk.
  • Transparent pricing. You see the range before you commit. No hidden fees.
  • Local talent. We work with Nigeria's best installers, upholsterers, painters and technicians.
  • Your car, your vision. We listen first, recommend second, and build exactly what you want.
