Frequently Asked Questions

Can you import a 2010 G-Wagon, rebuild it in China, and bring it back?

No. Nigeria's 12-year age limit and the prohibition on reassembled vehicles make that model illegal. We focus on locally available, compliant vehicles and legal restyling.

Do you take deposits on this website?

No. This site only collects interest. Any payment happens later, after inspection, a written scope, and a signed agreement.

How long does a restyle take?

It depends on parts availability. Simple upgrades can take days. Full body kits and interior work can take 2–6 weeks.

Is modifying a car legal in Nigeria?

Modifications must be disclosed to the licensing authority, and the car must remain roadworthy. We only work on legally registered vehicles and advise you on paperwork.

Can you guarantee a specific resale price?

No. Resale value depends on the market, documentation, condition and buyer. We do not promise any specific figure.

Which cities do you serve?

We serve clients nationwide. Our partner workshops are currently concentrated in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, but we can arrange logistics for other cities.

Do you source parts yourself or do I bring my own?

Both. We can source kits, wheels, lighting and tech through our trusted suppliers, or install parts you've already purchased. We'll advise on the best option for your budget.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer and cash. We typically require a deposit to order parts, with the balance due on inspection and approval of the finished work.
