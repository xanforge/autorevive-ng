GOOD TO KNOW

Ask early.
Decide clearly.

Not sure whether your idea is feasible? Start with an enquiry. The first step is to understand the vehicle and the intended work.

Ask about your project ↗
Can you modernise an older G-Wagon or another luxury vehicle?

We can discuss a proposed restyling plan, but feasibility depends on the exact model, generation, vehicle condition, compatible parts, workshop capability and applicable requirements. We do not guarantee a particular conversion before those checks.

Can you arrange a rebuild in China?

International sourcing or workshop coordination would require direct supplier verification, a written scope, shipping and customs advice, and confirmation that the proposed vehicle and work can be handled lawfully. Do not assume that an overseas supplier has accepted a project until you have written confirmation.

Can I request a quote before buying the vehicle?

Yes. Share the make, model, year and desired transformation. We can help identify the information needed for a preliminary feasibility and cost discussion, but a final quote may require inspection and supplier confirmation.

Are the vehicles in your gallery AutoRevive builds?

No, unless a specific item is explicitly labelled as a completed AutoRevive project. Gallery imagery is illustrative reference material used to communicate styles and ideas.

How much does a conversion cost?

There is no single reliable price for every vehicle. Cost depends on the base vehicle, parts, labour, paint or upholstery, shipping, taxes or duties where applicable, inspection and the precise scope. We aim to confirm an itemised estimate before commitment.

How long will a project take?

Timing depends on parts availability, workshop capacity, the amount of work and any logistics or approval steps. We will share an estimate only after the project details have been reviewed; delays may still occur.

Do you guarantee a resale price?

No. Resale value depends on the market, vehicle identity and condition, documentation, workmanship and buyer demand. A modernised appearance does not turn an older vehicle into a genuine newer model year.

Do I have to pay a deposit to send an enquiry?

No payment is required to submit the website enquiry. If a project proceeds, any deposit or milestone payment should be covered by a written agreement that identifies the recipient, scope, refund conditions and delivery terms.

What about Nigerian regulations and documentation?

Requirements depend on the vehicle and proposed work. Vehicle identity, registration, import status, roadworthiness, insurance and any required approvals should be checked with the relevant authorities or qualified professionals before proceeding. This website is not legal advice.

How do I get started?

Use the enquiry form with your vehicle details, the changes you want and your budget range. You can also contact us through WhatsApp to discuss the initial requirements.
