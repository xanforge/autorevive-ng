Our compliance commitment

Nigerian law sets a 12-year age limit on imported passenger vehicles and prohibits the import of reassembled vehicles. We work only with legally registered vehicles already in Nigeria and ensure every modification is roadworthy.

Modifications must be disclosed to the licensing authority within 14 days. We advise you on the paperwork and ensure your car remains insurable.
