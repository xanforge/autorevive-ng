THE PROCESS

Clear steps.
Fewer surprises.

We start with your vehicle and intended outcome, then work through compatibility, sourcing, costs and constraints before anything is confirmed.

01
DISCOVERY

Tell us what you have in mind.

Share your vehicle make, model, year, location, current condition and reference images. If you don't own the vehicle yet, explain what you intend to source.

Enquiry
02
FEASIBILITY

Check the details.

We identify the main compatibility questions, parts requirements, workshop needs and relevant documentation or compliance checks. Some projects may not be viable.

Assessment
03
SPECIFICATION

Agree what the build includes.

Review the proposed parts, labour, sourcing, logistics, exclusions, estimated timeline and payment milestones in writing. Estimates remain subject to supplier and vehicle confirmation.

Written scope
04
AUTHORISATION

Proceed only when the terms are clear.

Any order or work should begin only after the parties have agreed the written terms and confirmed the necessary arrangements. Don't rely on a social-media message as the whole contract.

Agreement
05
DELIVERY & REVIEW

Inspect against the agreed scope.

Confirm the work performed, parts supplied, documents and outstanding items against the agreement. Delivery dates depend on actual supplier, workshop and logistics conditions.

Handover
Important

AutoRevive NG does not guarantee a conversion outcome, import approval, resale value or delivery date before the specific project has been assessed and documented.
